System Setup & Data Management

Initialize system data, manage chart of accounts, and configure product attributes

About System Setup

These setup tasks initialize required system data for your inventory management system. They are designed to be idempotent - safe to run multiple times without duplicating data.

First-time setup: Run all tasks to initialize your system
Safe updates: Re-run tasks to add missing accounts or attributes
No duplicates: Existing data will not be overwritten
Customizable: View and edit data after initialization

Chart of Accounts

General Ledger accounts

Initialize the chart of accounts with standard GL accounts for accrual accounting.

What This Does:

  • Creates asset accounts: Cash (1000), Accounts Receivable (1200), Inventory (1300)
  • Creates liability accounts: Accounts Payable (2000), Unearned Revenue (2300)
  • Creates income accounts: Sales Revenue (4000), Other Income (4800)
  • Creates expense accounts: COGS (5000), Operating Expenses (6000-6900)

Inventory GL Balance

Sync inventory with GL

One-time

Create an opening balance journal entry to sync your inventory value with the General Ledger.

What This Does:

  • Calculates total value of current inventory (quantity × purchase price)
  • Creates opening balance journal entry: Dr Inventory (1300), Cr Equity
  • Syncs your physical stock with accounting records
