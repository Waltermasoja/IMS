## 🎨 Design System Applied

- **Colors**: Blue primary (#3b82f6), with semantic colors for success, warning, danger
- **Typography**: Inter font family
- **Spacing**: Tailwind's default scale (4, 6, 8, 12px)
- **Borders**: Rounded-2xl (16px) for cards, rounded-xl (12px) for inputs/buttons
- **Shadows**: Subtle shadows with hover effects
- **Animations**: Fade-in, slide-in, scale transitions with Alpine.js
